ABOUT

ESCAPE is an urban hotel overlooking the sea and the city.
Its name reflects the idea at the heart of the hotel:
making room for a different kind of experience, one shaped by
the surroundings and the freedom to enjoy the city in your own way.

At ESCAPE, the view is never quite the same.
The city stretches out below, the sea opens up beyond it,
and the rooftop pool offers a front-row seat to both.
The hotel is designed to make the most of this setting,
with the landscape remaining part of the experience from
the first moments of the morning until the city lights come on at night.
